# Company Policies

## Free Consultation Policy

Every new client is eligible for a free 30-minute consultancy call with one of our experts. No obligation. The call covers understanding your requirements, providing initial recommendations, and discussing next steps.

## NDA Policy

We are happy to sign an NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) before discussing project details. Just request it and we'll accommodate.

## Payment Terms

Standard payment terms: 40% project start, 30% milestone, 30% delivery. Monthly retainers are invoiced at the start of each month. Hosting fees are billed quarterly or annually. Exact terms are defined in the project proposal.

## Development Methodology

We follow an agile-inspired approach: Discovery phase to define scope, iterative development with weekly/bi-weekly demos, continuous testing, and structured deployment. Clients receive regular updates and have full visibility into progress.

## Post-Launch Support

We offer post-launch support packages including bug fixes, minor enhancements, monitoring, and uptime guarantees. Support SLA is defined in the maintenance agreement.

## Data Privacy & GDPR

Loggix is fully GDPR compliant. We offer private data hosting in Dutch and European data centers with 24/7 monitoring, automated backups, and strict access controls. Client data never leaves the EU without explicit consent.

## Escalation Process

If a caller requests a manager, team lead, or the founder (Jeroen Lutmers), collect their name, email, and inquiry, and the appropriate person will reach out within 1 business day.

## Intellectual Property

All custom code and IP developed for the client belongs to the client upon full payment. We retain the right to use anonymized project details in our portfolio.
